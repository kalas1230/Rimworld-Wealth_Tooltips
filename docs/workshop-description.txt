Release\upload\description-paste.txt from everything below the divider; paste that file, not
this one. Keep it short on purpose: the Workshop listing is read at a glance, and the fuller

Steam BBCode is supported ([b], [i], [list]) but plain text reads better at this length.

==========

Hover a row in the resource readout. See what it is worth.

The tooltip adds three things: the item or category's value in silver, its share of your colony's
total wealth, and how much of it is stored versus sitting elsewhere; on the ground, in pawn
inventories.

Wealth drives raid size, and the readout alone will not tell you which pile is doing the damage.
There are better mods suited for this task if this is what you are looking for.
This mod puts the answer where you are already looking, without opening a window.

Tooltips only. Nothing is added to the panel, nothing moves, nothing is resized. The wealth figure
is vanilla's own.

Safe to add to any save and safe to remove from any save: the mod writes nothing into your save
file. Works in both the categorized and simple readout modes. Requires Harmony.
